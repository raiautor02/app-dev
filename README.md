# Spiderman Across The Spider Verse
## 2023
### Miles Morales
**I like *Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse* because it has an amazing story, beautiful animation, and incredible characters. What makes the movie special to me is Miles Morales, because he shows that being a hero is not about following everyone else's expectations but about believing in yourself and choosing your own path. I also love how the movie combines different Spider-Man characters, unique art styles, and exciting action scenes that make every moment enjoyable. The emotional moments, especially Miles' struggles with his family and his identity, make the story even more meaningful. Overall, *Across the Spider-Verse* is one of my favorite movies because it inspires me to be myself, overcome challenges, and never give up on what I believe in.**
